# 📊 Military Expenditure Analysis Dashboard

This repository contains a detailed Power BI analysis comparing the military expenditures of **India** and **Pakistan** from **1960 to 2023**. The dashboard visualizes trends in absolute spending, percentage of GDP, and strategic ratios over time to understand the fiscal and defense priorities of both nations.

---

## 📌 Key Features

- 📈 **Time-Series Trends**: Military expenditure from 1960–2023
- 🔍 **Country Comparison**: India vs Pakistan
  - Total military spending (US$)
  - % of GDP allocated to military
  - Spending ratio over time
- 🧮 **Decade-wise Aggregates**
- 🌐 **GDP Contextualization**: Overlay of GDP growth for correlation

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### Total Military Expenditure (1960–2023)
- 🇮🇳 **India**: $1.32 Trillion
- 🇵🇰 **Pakistan**: $237.86 Billion

### 2023 Spending
- India: **$83.57B**
- Pakistan: **$8.52B**

### Military Spending as % of GDP
- India: **2.44% (avg)**
- Pakistan: **2.80% (avg)**  
  *(Pakistan's ratio peaked at 6–7% in some decades)*

### Spending Ratio (India : Pakistan)
- Ranged from **2.4x to 9.8x**
- Indicates widening military investment gap

## 🧠 Strategic Takeaways

- **India**: Balanced defense investment aligned with GDP growth; strategic focus on modernization and self-reliance (e.g., HAL Tejas, DRDO, Make in India).
- **Pakistan**: Higher military spending as % of GDP despite economic constraints; reflects prioritization of national security.
